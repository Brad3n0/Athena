Chatting

Every tab
Three tabs
Assistant, Code and Study. Each tab keeps its own chat when you switch.
✨ Auto
In Assistant, she picks the best model for each message: code, math and school, pictures or general chat.
Think button
⚡ Quick, Normal or 🧠 Deep. The 🧠 under an answer redoes just that one with Deep thinking.
Answer style
Classic or Polished. Settings → General.
Reply length & Direct mode
Short, Normal or Detailed. Direct mode skips lecturing and needless warnings.
Regenerate & versions
Get a new answer and flip between versions with ‹ ›.
Edit your message
Change a message you sent and resend it in place.
Branch
Start a new chat from any answer. The original stays as it was.
Compare models
Two models answer side by side. Keep the better one.
Pictures
Attach photos, worksheets or screenshots, or let her look at your screen.
Saved replies
Star any answer to keep it.
Search everything
Search every chat and every message from the sidebar.
Projects
Folders of chats with shared instructions and files.
Pin, rename, export
Tidy chats from the sidebar. Titles get an emoji automatically.
Slash commands
Type / to see them all.

Deep research

Research button
🔭 Research
She plans searches, reads about a dozen pages, fills gaps, then writes a report with numbered citations and sources.
Watch her work
A live panel shows each search, each page she reads or skips, and what she found. Takes a few minutes.
Quick web search
For a fast answer from the web.

Athena learns

Settings → About you
Remembers you
Picks up lasting things you mention: your grade, hobbies, projects, likes.
👍 / 👎
Rate answers and say what to do better. It becomes a rule she follows in every chat.
Corrections
Say “no, I meant…” and she learns from it.
About you page
See, edit, add or delete everything she's learned, or turn learning off.

Voice

Voice button
Voice orb
Talk back and forth. The orb shows her mood. Talk over her to interrupt.
Natural offline voice
Kokoro voice, or your Windows voices.
Dictation
The mic button types what you say.
Voice commands
“slower”, “faster”, “stop”, “new chat” and more.
Other languages
Talk in your language and she answers in it.
Hey Athena
Wake word and hotkeys, with the desktop app.

Study

Study tab
Homework help
Step by step, with the working shown.
Explain my mistake
Show your work and she finds where it went wrong.
Quizzes & flashcards
Made from any topic or your notes.
Decks & review
Saved decks with spaced review of what's due.
Graphs
Plots functions and data right in the chat.
Cheat sheets
One printable page on any topic.
Study streak 🔥
Counts the days in a row you study.
Focus timer
A timed study session.

Code

Code tab
Live previews
Websites, games and apps run right in the chat, or in a new tab.
Run code
Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, PowerShell, Batch and Bash.
Project folders
Open a real folder. She reads it, shows a diff and asks before each change.
Builds like a developer
Creates the project, writes the files, runs them, screenshots the result and fixes problems.
Upload to GitHub
Publishes a finished project. Asks first.

Writing canvas

Canvas button
Write together
Essays, letters and stories side by side with the chat.
Edit tools
Proofread, make it shorter or more formal, print or export.

Jarvis: your PC

Settings → Jarvis
Apps & controls
Open apps, volume, media keys, lock, clipboard, screenshots.
Messages
Discord, WhatsApp, texts and email to saved contacts. Always asks before sending.
Windows
Focus, minimize, maximize, snap left or right, close, move to another monitor.
Type & click
Types text for you and clicks things on screen.
Routines
One phrase runs several steps.
PC status
CPU, graphics card, memory, disks, network and the busiest apps.
Heads-ups
Download finished, PC maxed out, drive nearly full, low battery, reminder coming up.

Files & web

Settings → Abilities
Find files
Anywhere in your folders.
Organize folders
Sorts a messy folder into groups. Asks before moving or deleting.
Your documents
Answers from files in your Knowledge folders.
Weather
Now and the next 3 days.

Sports

Any chat
Scores & lines
Tonight's games, live scores, spreads, totals and moneylines.
Check a pick
For PrizePicks, Underdog or any prop: how often the player went over that line (last 5, last 10, season, last season, home/away, vs the opponent), plus minutes and injuries.
Teams & history
Record, form, head-to-head, injuries, news, and past seasons.
Kalshi prices
Live market odds for a team or game (read-only).
Honest by design
Shows what has actually happened and what the market thinks. No one can promise wins.

Everyday

Sidebar
Tasks
A to-do list she can add to and check off.
Reminders & timers
Pop up and speak when it's time.
Morning briefing
Weather, tasks and reminders for the day.
Images
With the optional Stable Diffusion add-on.
Smart home
With the optional Home Assistant add-on.
