              👥 Contacts

              Who's who when you say "message Jake". Add their exact usernames so she opens the right chat (Discord, Instagram, Snapchat, Telegram). A phone number is needed for WhatsApp and texts, and an email address for email.

              🔔 Heads-ups

              She tells you when something needs your attention (while Athena is open).

              🖥 PC status

              Run Athena as a desktop app: she lives in the system tray, opens in her own window, and pops up with a shortcut from anywhere. Start it with start-desktop.bat.

              Format: <ctrl>+<space>, <ctrl>+<alt>+a, <cmd>+<shift>+a. Takes effect right away in the desktop app.

              📱 Use Athena on your phone

              Chat with Athena from your phone while she runs on this PC. Your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi. Everything still runs on the PC, so "open Spotify" opens it on the PC.

              Tries every part of Athena and shows what's working. If something shows ✗, the note says how to fix it.
